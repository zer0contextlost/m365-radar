M365 Radar tracks the public [Microsoft 365 Roadmap](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/roadmap)
and, for changes likely to matter operationally, pairs the announcement with
a short PowerShell snippet you can run against your own tenant to check
whether it affects you.

**Every script here is read-only.** They use `Get-*` cmdlets only — Microsoft
Graph PowerShell SDK or Exchange Online Management — and never `Set-`,
`Remove-`, `New-`, `Add-`, `Update-`, `Enable-`, `Disable-`, `Revoke-`, or
`Install-`. Nothing on this site changes anything in your tenant. Read a
script before you run it, same as you would for anything else pasted from
the internet — that's on you, not this disclaimer.

Content is grounded in Microsoft's own published roadmap entries — feature
ID, status, and rollout timing come from the roadmap item itself, not from
general knowledge or guesswork about what a change probably does.
